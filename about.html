Environmental Stewardship

Sustainable Media for Smart Cities

We believe that large-scale public networks have a responsibility to local communities. AdVantage is dedicated to reducing the carbon and visual footprint of OOH through sustainable architecture and intelligent screen operations.

  • Low-Power LED Diodes: All digital bulletins deploy next-generation light modules reducing electrical grids strain.
  • Ambient Luminosity Sensors: Screens monitor regional daylight cycles to auto-dim and avoid urban light pollution.
  • Solar Transit Panels: Suburban transit street shelters run on localized solar battery arrays.
