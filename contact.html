Frequently Asked Questions

Contact & RFP FAQs

Have questions about starting a billboard campaign or submitting an RFP? Find quick answers below.

How long does it take to receive a custom campaign proposal after submitting the form?
Our OOH strategists typically analyze your target coordinates and budget parameters to deliver a comprehensive route mapping and availability plan within 24 to 48 business hours.
What information is required to get an accurate billboard advertising quote?
To provide the most precise inventory pricing and reach estimation, we need your target metropolitan regions, preferred media formats (e.g., highway bulletins, digital screens, bus shelters), estimated campaign duration, and target launch date.
Can I change my ad creatives mid-campaign if I submit an RFP?
Yes! For our programmatic digital billboard displays (DOOH), creatives can be updated remotely and launched within less than 5 minutes. For physical wraps (such as transit bus wraps or printed vinyl bulletins), creative updates require additional printing and installation scheduling.
Is there a minimum budget required to start advertising with AdVantage?
We accommodate campaigns starting at $1,000 for localized digital billboard slots. For broader transit packages, subway networks, or premier Times Square placements, minimum budgets vary based on geographic density and duration.
